Research experience

2022 – present
Graduate Student Researcher, University of California, Davis
  • Own a project end to end, from experimental design to analysis: built a Python/PyTorch simulation pipeline with SLURM job arrays covering 1,060 parameter combinations for three recurrent network models (up to N = 2,000), generating about 22,000 runs over nearly 90,000 CPU core-hours.
